    Supported CSV and counting rules

    • The first non-ignored record is the header and is excluded from all data-row, blank-data-cell, and duplicate counts. No header-name validation is performed.
    • UTF-8 with an optional BOM, commas, LF or CRLF line endings, quoted fields, doubled quote escapes, and embedded commas/newlines in quoted fields are supported.
    • Completely empty lines outside quoted fields are ignored. Whitespace-only lines are records, not ignored lines. Line breaks inside quotes are preserved.
    • Empty or whitespace-only data cells are blank. Cell contents are never trimmed or case-folded for duplicate detection. Duplicate rows compare exact parsed cell values; each occurrence after the first counts once.
    • Ragged rows and malformed quoting are structural errors. Counts are withheld on any structural error. Locations use logical records (including header) and physical lines (including ignored empty lines). An empty file has zero rows and columns.

    Limitations

    No duplicate removal, delimiter guessing, XLSX, alternate encodings, or large-file guarantees. Entire input and duplicate keys are kept in memory; large files can freeze the tab. Bare CR line endings, quotes in unquoted cells, and spaces after a closing quote are unsupported. Valid one-column text is also CSV: passing these checks does not prove a file is safe or its values are meaningful.
